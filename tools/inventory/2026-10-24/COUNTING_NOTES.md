# What these counts mean

108 is the historical accepted baseline: 99 Bob builds plus nine Combo-Breaker suites. There are 106 formal receipts and two pending suite receipts. A source audit found 167 discovery identity rows, not 167 proven unique implementations.

This catalogue exposes 285 descriptive records: 167 registry identities, 77 research-only ideas, 23 Revenue Lab app records, 12 infrastructure/component/suite records and six already-published derivatives. Three Revenue rows now link to new scoped-MIT source handoffs, bringing public source IDs to nine. The 276 remaining rows are descriptions, not downloadable finished tools.

These groups are not a sum of distinct tools. Suites reuse components, public packages derive from private templates, historical BOB IDs collide across implementations, and cross-collection semantic overlap is unresolved. The exact current all-time distinct implementation count is unknown. No source or historical version was deleted.

The research reviewed registries, receipts and manifests, not every full artifact. Historical tests are labelled with their scope; documentation inspection is not a runtime test. Potential and contribution effort labels are editorial. Missing or unindexed work can remain outside this bounded catalogue.

Only the nine exact IDs in the repository CONTRIBUTING.md have scoped manual intake. Metadata-only listing is not source reuse permission, a promise of source availability or approval to submit unrelated code. The newest three sources retain a documented CSV parser defect. Current browser and fresh outsider pickup-to-release acceptance are not established.
