Date: November 12, 2026. **SCREENING NOTE, NOT TRADEMARK CLEARANCE OR LEGAL OPINION.**

This document accompanies dogfooding of an experimental pilot. A publicly readable draft PR is not a product release or proof of trademark ownership.

## Observations and risks

- The short word **WIT** is already used publicly by unrelated technology businesses, including the established developer service **Wit.ai** and **WIT Software** (telecom/agentic AI). We should not assume exclusivity in the short name.
- Preliminary public web searches did not identify a clear independent use of **Witburrow** beyond this project's pages. Search non-results do not prove availability; official Canadian, U.S. and international trademark registers have **not** been exhaustively searched for close spelling, sound, design or related services.
- **Syns**, **Claude**, **Codex**, **Gemini**, **Trellis**, and other providers/projects have their own brands. Descriptive attribution is appropriate; do not imply endorsement, licensing partnership or affiliation.

## No-cost mitigation for this pilot

1. Use **Witburrow Continuity Observer** as the full prominent product label. Treat **WIT** as a secondary nickname or historical identifier, not a claim to exclusive rights.
2. Retain technical IDs, version pins, Git paths, download references and existing release hashes; this is outward-facing wording only.
3. Never apply a registered-mark symbol or claim official trademark registration/clearance without evidence.
4. Keep vendor names in accurate attribution, source-provenance or interoperability descriptions, not in WIT's own product title, logo or endorsement copy.
5. Before any *new broad brand launch*, complete live official-register searches for exact and confusingly similar terms and relevant software/services in intended jurisdictions (CIPO Canada, USPTO U.S., WIPO international). Confirm current status and commercial use; third-party cached listings alone are not enough.
6. If a material conflict is verified, hold affected uses and prepare a genuinely distinct mark under owner review. Do not imitate branding or rely on a trivial spelling change to sidestep rights.
